# Sponsorship Policy

Infrastructure Heroes accepts financial support to fund its work. Because the
project publishes health assessments of open-source projects and the vendors
around them, independence is a core requirement, not a courtesy.

## Principles

1. **No influence over results.** Sponsors cannot influence health scores,
   rankings, coverage, category placement, wording, or the decision to include
   or exclude a project.
2. **Metrics stay automated and public.** Scores come from public data via the
   documented methodology and the weekly automation pipeline. Sponsors receive
   no preview, no override, and no privileged access to data.
3. **Sponsors are disclosed.** Supporters are listed on the Thank Wall and on
   the project's Open Collective page. Sponsorship is never presented as an
   endorsement of a sponsor's product or service, and no sponsored content is
   published as analysis.
4. **No strings attached.** Sponsorship agreements do not include editorial
   review, approval rights, exclusivity, or a say in the roadmap. We may
   decline or return a sponsorship that conflicts with the project's mission
   or independence.
5. **Funding pays for project work.** Funds cover completed, invoiced work:
   the metrics pipeline, research and writing of project and maintainer
   profiles, site and data infrastructure, and outreach. When funds are held
   by a fiscal host (e.g. Open Source Collective), their expense policies also
   apply.
6. **Corrections are independent.** Data corrections follow the same public
   process regardless of who reports them.

## Scope

This policy covers GitHub Sponsors, Open Collective, direct corporate
sponsorships, and in-kind support for Infrastructure Heroes itself. Donations
made to an individual maintainer through a link on a profile page are not
sponsorships of this project and are outside this policy; those links are
provided as a convenience and imply no endorsement.

## Changes

This policy may be updated as the project grows. Every change is committed
publicly to this repository.
