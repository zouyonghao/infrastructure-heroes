## Infrastructure contributions

Igor Sysoev began developing Nginx in 2002. On January 18, 2022, NGINX announced that he was stepping back from NGINX and F5. This profile recognizes his historical contribution rather than presenting him as the current maintainer. [NGINX's announcement](https://blog.nginx.org/blog/do-svidaniya-igor-thank-you-for-nginx).
