### Overview

FFmpeg is a complete, cross-platform solution to record, convert, and stream audio and video. It is used by virtually every video application.

### Importance

- Powers video processing everywhere
- Foundation of streaming services
- Critical for media applications
- Used by YouTube, VLC, and more

### Key Features

- Codec support (hundreds)
- Transcoding
- Streaming
- Filtering

### Sustainability

FFmpeg documents separate routes for donations to the project and gifts to individual developers. [FFmpeg donation guidance](https://ffmpeg.org/donations.html). [FFmpeg developer documentation](https://ffmpeg.org/developer.html).
